# Privacy Policy: MultiList for TradingView

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

MultiList for TradingView ("the extension") is an unofficial tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to TradingView.

## 1. Data stored in your browser

- Your groups, tabs, and symbols are stored in Chrome's sync storage. They sync with your browser account.
- Your API keys are stored only on your device. They are never sent to our servers.

## 2. Quote requests to third-party sources

The extension requests quotes directly from your browser. These requests do not pass through our servers. The sources are:

- TWSE MIS (mis.twse.com.tw)
- Yahoo Finance
- Binance
- Fugle
- Alpaca
- The TradingView website

These services receive the symbols being looked up. They handle that data under their own privacy policies.

## 3. Optional data sharing

Sharing is off until you press "Agree" in the extension's consent window. You can turn it off at any time in the extension's Settings.

If you agree, the extension may send two kinds of data:

**a. Install registration**

- A randomly generated anonymous install ID
- The first install date
- The extension version

**b. List changes**

- A randomly generated anonymous analytics ID
- Each event of adding or removing a symbol (time, symbol, random group ID)
- With each upload, the current symbol list of each group (symbols and random group IDs only)
- The extension version

Details:

- Upload frequency: about once every 7 days, while your browser is open.
- The two IDs are sent separately and stored separately. They are not linked to each other.

## 4. What we do not collect

- Group names
- Tab names
- API keys
- Browsing history
- Email address
- Your name

We do not store IP addresses.

## 5. How we use the data

- To provide and improve the extension, including aggregated market-trend features that may be added to the extension later.
- We do not sell the data.
- We do not use it for advertising.
- We do not transfer it to third parties, except to our hosting provider (Google Cloud) and where required by law.
- People only look at aggregated, anonymized statistics.

The use of data received from the extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## 6. Retention and turning sharing off

- Shared data is kept indefinitely.
- Turning sharing off stops future uploads and deletes any records in the extension that have not been sent yet.
- Data that was already sent stays on our side. It is anonymous and cannot be traced back to you.

## 7. Where data is stored

Shared data is stored on Google Cloud (Firestore, Taiwan region, asia-east1).

## 8. Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13.

## 9. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the effective date. If we change the types of data we collect, the extension asks for your consent again.
