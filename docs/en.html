MultiList for TradingView 繁體中文

A TradingView watchlist with no watchlist limits

MultiList for TradingView is a free browser extension that shows your watchlist in the side panel. Create as many groups as you want: they do not count against your TradingView watchlist limits. Put the same symbol in many sectors to follow sector rotation, and click a symbol to switch your TradingView chart to it.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store, where Chrome, Edge and Brave can all install it. Free, and no account needed.

Features

No TradingView watchlist limits

Create as many groups as you need. MultiList keeps its own lists, so they do not count against your TradingView watchlist limits.

One symbol, many sectors

Keep TSMC in "Semiconductors", "AI" and "Large caps" at once. Make one group for each sector, and compare them to follow sector rotation.

Click to switch the chart

Click a symbol in your list, and your open TradingView chart switches to it. The page does not reload, so unsaved chart changes stay.

Search by ticker or name

Type a ticker or a company name, in English or Chinese, to find a symbol. Results come from TradingView search.

Import your TradingView watchlists

Read your lists from your signed-in TradingView tab. Each list becomes a tab, and each section becomes a group.

Stock and crypto prices

See the last price, change and change % (you choose the columns), plus US pre-market and after-hours prices. Markets: Taiwan stocks (TWSE, TPEx), US, European and Korean stocks (KOSPI, KOSDAQ), and crypto on Binance, Bybit, OKX, Bitget, Gate, KuCoin and MEXC.

Tabs and drag to reorder

Organize groups in tabs. Drag symbols and groups to reorder them, or drag a symbol to another group.

Sync across computers and browsers

In Chrome, your list syncs through your Chrome account. Sign in with Google to also sync it across computers and browsers.

Export, import and custom colors

Export your list as a JSON file to back it up or share it. Use the light or dark theme, or pick your own background and text colors.

Get started

  1. Install MultiList for TradingView from the Chrome Web Store, in Chrome, Edge or Brave.
  2. Click the MultiList icon in the browser toolbar to open the side panel.
  3. Press Import TradingView lists to bring in your lists, or press Add symbol to add symbols yourself.
  4. Open a TradingView chart, and click a symbol in your list to switch the chart.

The interface is in English and Traditional Chinese. It follows your browser language, and you can switch it in the ⋯ menu.

FAQ

What is MultiList for TradingView?

MultiList for TradingView is a free browser extension that shows your own watchlist in the side panel. You can create unlimited groups that do not count against TradingView watchlist limits, and put the same symbol in many groups. Click a symbol to switch your TradingView chart to it.

Do TradingView watchlist limits apply to MultiList?

No. MultiList keeps its own lists, so they do not count against the number of TradingView watchlists or symbols. According to the TradingView pricing page (checked in November 2026), the free Basic plan has 1 watchlist with 30 symbols. MultiList has no limit on groups and holds about 1,500 to 2,000 symbols in total.

Is there a limit on groups or symbols?

MultiList itself does not limit the number of groups. Your list is stored in the browser's sync storage, which is limited to about 100 KB in total and 8 KB per group. With typical symbols and names, that is about 1,500 to 2,000 symbols in total and up to about 170 symbols in one group. The exact number depends on the length of the names.

Can the same symbol be in more than one group?

Yes. For example, put TSMC in "Semiconductors", "AI" and "Large caps" at once. Make one group for each sector, and compare the moves in each group to follow sector rotation.

Is it free? Do I need a paid TradingView plan?

MultiList is free, and it works with a free TradingView account. Switching the chart and searching need an open TradingView tab. The import also needs you to be signed in to TradingView.

How is a MultiList list different from a TradingView watchlist?

A MultiList list is stored in your browser. It never changes the watchlists in your TradingView account. The import only reads your TradingView lists and copies them into MultiList.

Which browsers does it support?

Desktop browsers based on Chromium, such as Chrome, Edge and Brave. All of them install it from the Chrome Web Store. It is tested mainly in Chrome, and we checked that it loads and runs in Brave. Firefox, Safari and mobile browsers are not supported.

Can I sync my list to other computers or browsers?

Yes. In Chrome, computers signed in to the same Chrome account sync the list automatically. Sign in to MultiList with Google to also sync it across computers and browsers.

Which markets have prices?

Taiwan stocks (TWSE, TPEx), US stocks, European stocks, Korean stocks (KOSPI, KOSDAQ), crypto spot pairs and perpetual contracts on Binance, Bybit, OKX, Bitget, Gate and MEXC, and KuCoin spot pairs. US stocks also show pre-market and after-hours prices during those sessions. You can also add other symbols, such as futures, indices or forex. They show no price, but a click still switches the chart.

Are the prices real-time?

Prices are for reference only. They come from sources such as TWSE MIS, Yahoo Finance, Binance and Bybit, and can be delayed or wrong.

How do I import my TradingView watchlists?

Open TradingView in your browser and sign in. Then press 一鍵匯入 TradingView 清單 (Import from TradingView) in the side panel. Each TradingView list becomes a tab, and each section becomes a group. The import adds to your current list and never replaces it.

Does MultiList collect my data?

If you do not sign in and do not turn on anonymous sharing, no data is sent to the MultiList server. Google sign-in and anonymous sharing are both optional. See the privacy policy for details.

Is MultiList an official TradingView product?

No. MultiList is a third-party tool by an independent developer, not an official TradingView product.

Before you use it

  • Search, chart switching and the import run in your TradingView tab. If TradingView changes its site, these features can stop working for a while.
  • Search and the import use internal TradingView endpoints that are not documented, and the TradingView terms restrict this. Use it at your own risk. See the README for details.
  • It works only in desktop browsers based on Chromium, such as Chrome, Edge and Brave.
  • Prices are for reference only and are not investment advice.
